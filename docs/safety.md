# Safety and content governance

No diagnosis, prescription, autonomous triage, pregnancy risk score, emergency dispatch, or child registration. Adult confirmation and registration consent are mandatory. Referral reasons are non-diagnostic controlled categories; clinician-selected urgency is restricted to clinic coordination. The tool cannot ensure access to transport, staff or actual care.

The fixed safety statement directs urgent concerns to qualified care or local emergency services without asserting a country-specific number or clinically approved danger-sign list. Those resources must be supplied and signed off by a health partner before real deployment.

English seed templates are **illustrative synthetic demo sources**. Approval flags demonstrate workflow only; no qualified clinician review is claimed. Deterministic simplification is labeled honestly and produces an unapproved draft with source/model/version metadata. A demo clinic coordinator may record a demo review. No identifiable patient prompt or open-ended medical question is accepted. Twi, Ewe and Dagbani are preference keys only until qualified translations are supplied.

Before a real content release, store reviewer identity and verified credentials, clinical source/copyright permission, locale, version, expiry/review date and rollback history. Review wording, lock-screen privacy, emergency guidance and referral protocol. Disable problematic copy, inform the clinical/operational owner, identify affected deliveries without exposing PHI in tickets, record corrections, and re-review translations.

Metrics show process activity only: contact attempts, reached contacts, referral closure and registration withdrawal. They do not establish improved clinical outcomes or reduced mortality. Cohort exclusions and denominators are displayed; missing outcomes and empty denominators remain visible.
