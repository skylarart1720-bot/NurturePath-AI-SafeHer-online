## Suggested milestone sequence

1. Engineering completion and local synthetic verification: finish this release and review its evidence.
2. Protected Vercel staging: configure account, separate database, region, stable hostname and secrets; test deployment/restore without real-user data.
3. Professional and operational reviews: complete named owner gates, content/provider validation, independent security and adult accessibility studies; remediate findings.
4. Authorized adult educational pilot: approve a release and limited protocol with supported languages/coverage; monitor incidents, review expiry and deletion recovery.
5. Later referrals or regional expansion: require new agreements, consent/security workflows, local review and a separately tested release.

Dates, budget amounts, team biographies, public repository/demo links, application eligibility and deadline must be supplied/verified before submission. No impact or partnership claims are generated from synthetic metrics.

## Manual accessibility and usability protocol

Recruit consenting adults only under an approved protocol. Do not solicit personal abuse/health disclosures. Test the five demo journeys using fictional scenarios on low-end Android, iOS, a tablet, keyboard-only navigation, and at least one screen reader. Check headings/landmarks, labels, focus order/visibility, zoom/reflow, touch targets, status announcements, directory consent, Quick Exit behavior/trace explanation, downloaded content removal/expiry, and reduced motion. Record device/browser/assistive technology versions, task success/time, accessibility findings and remediation. Obtain reviewer acceptance. Automated axe checks are supporting evidence, not WCAG certification.

## Threat review agenda

Review account/MFA invitation theft and replay, insecure role promotion, session revocation races, partner ownership changes, content/source injection and stale review, gateway vendor exfiltration, directory poisoning/false availability, hostile Origin/proxy headers, chunked oversized bodies, rate-limit evasion, offline browser sharing/clock changes, deletion resurrection after backup restore, log leakage, and exposed legacy clinical routes. Record likelihood/impact, accountable mitigation, verification and residual risk. Independent review remains required.
